About GSNOOK

Scientific depth translated into useful deliverables

GSNOOK is an independent biomedical consultancy founded by Gonzalo Illán. It combines medical translation and documentation expertise with a long research career and advanced training in bioinformatics and biostatistics.

Gonzalo holds a PhD in Animal Health and completed postdoctoral research in the United States. His background spans more than twenty years of biomedical research, teaching and scientific communication, together with more than four years of professional work in medical documentation and translation.

The aim is practical: to help Pharma, Biotech and CRO teams turn technically complex biomedical information into accurate, structured and decision-ready documentation, analyses and visual outputs.

Approach

Clear scope, traceable work and usable outputs

Each engagement is shaped around the client’s existing data, documentation, terminology and quality requirements.

1 · Define

Clarify purpose, audience, source materials, deliverables and review criteria.

2 · Assess

Review terminology, data readiness, methodological context and limitations.

3 · Deliver

Produce accurate bilingual documentation, analysis, reporting or visual outputs.

4 · Review

Resolve feedback, document decisions and provide practical final files.
